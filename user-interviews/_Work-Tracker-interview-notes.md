## Argot Collective (Solidity)  
**Category:** Infra/dev tool

Sov first-pass candidate. Interview complete 2026-07-16 (Lea, Mahesh attended). Signal: discovery fully relationship-based (conferences, referrals, large funders as de facto hubs), zero tooling. Post-grant sustainability is the driver: multi-year runway finalized (later years gated on progress), public-goods funding deteriorating, so Argot piloting scoped RFP-style work bundles + co-funders (EthDebug pilot; prospective co-funders in play). Hub asks: surface ecosystem/stakeholder priorities and roadmaps as a layer above RFPs; deadline notifications (Telegram cluttered); profile data they can contribute = transparency report, roadmap, Sourcify usage, annual Solidity dev survey. Neutrality tension: paid feature requests risk sidelining community roadmap (prior ecosystem precedent); floated a bounty-marketplace for funder-attached engineering resources but Solidity onboarding too heavy. Pain: AI-generated PR + security-report flood since ~Feb slowing the team down, no filtering tooling. Follow-ups: TG group created; Lea adds a Sourcify teammate Monday 7/20 to explore builder-profile metrics; Lea sharing EthDebug co-funding doc for Sov feedback. Funder-side thread (Argot publishing RFPs) stays separate.

## Cactus (fmrly ScopeLift)  
**Category:** Professional services

Sov first-pass candidate (doc comments). Intro: Sov. Outreach sent ~2026-07-07. Interview complete 2026-07-15 (Ben + Hugo, Mahesh attended). Signal: scoping cost is the pain, not discovery (LOE vs budget rarely inferable from the RFP, hours of preliminary discovery each; can't be fully automated, constraint is access to info/people). Publisher disorganization hurts. Overspecified RFPs a failure mode too. #1 ask (Ben): transparent in-platform Q&A with the publisher during the application window, default in the process. Daily-use driver (Hugo): comprehensive, visibly active data, not platform features. Wants counterparty-quality signal (technical stakeholders, pays on time, scope-creep risk); skeptical of anonymous publisher ratings (deanonymization trivial). Interface: agent/API + email/Telegram alerts. Meta: selection-effect skepticism, many RFPs effectively decided in advance toward an incumbent; treats RFPs as foot-in-door into new ecosystems; prefers long-term relationships over transactional bids (echoes CoBuilders). Willing to test the hub.

## CoBuilders
