# Adedalapo (BitGifty) — Builder Interview

**Category:** Builder

### **Core**

1. Walk me through how you currently find funding opportunities. What tools, what cadence?   
   For BitGifty, we basically try to get recommendations for grant opportunities via word of mouth from fellow builders with whom we are familiar. This is simply how we’ve gotten the grants we’ve been privileged to get. For our equity funds, we were privileged to be a part of Minipays’ ecosystem; this gave us access to our investor.  Besides this, we haven’t used any tool to find funding opportunities.   
     
2. Where does that process break down? What costs you the most time?

	The process breaks down at the application stage. Filling the application form for the fund is what takes the most time. Of course, the process can be tiring because there’s always a chance of not getting the grant so sometimes completing the application for these funds drags on for days. 

3. What information is most often missing or wrong in opportunity listings?   
   I have checked out some platforms that list some funding opportunities, but I’ve always been discouraged from taking them seriously. It’s hard to trust that they’re legitimate and worth the time. I might be wrong, though.  
     
4. If a neutral, open hub existed, what would it need to do for you to use it weekly?

	This would be perfect, especially for builders like us building from Africa that don’t have easy access to proper funding. I think there has to be a trust element. I have to be able to trust the hub to know that I’m not just wasting my time filling out these applications. Also, the forms should contain important questions, not just random inquiries meant to simply bore out the applicant. 

I think those are the 2 important factors for me. If I genuinely believe I could get access to funds and it’s not filled with too many unnecessary fields of questions, I’ll actually keep the tab open on my browser.

5. Which fields actually matter to you in a listing? (e.g. deadline, budget, ecosystem, eligibility, contact, status)

	Eligibility matters to me more. As a builder, my time is very valuable, and I wouldn’t want to spend it applying just to find out that we weren’t eligible. Besides eligibility, status, budget, deadline and then ecosystem in that order.

6. Would you contribute data back (submissions, corrections, flags)? What would make that worth doing?  
   Of course. If my data would help in making a dream hub for funding opportunities for everyone, I’d definitely contribute, no strings attached.   
7. How do you find opportunities today? What tools do you use?  
   As I mentioned above, I mostly just get word of mouth from my fellow builders.  
8. How do you decide whether an opportunity is worth applying to? What kills interest?  
   Due to the way I know about these opportunities, most of the funds I’ve applied for were worth applying for regardless of whether we got a positive or negative outcome. On the few occasions where I decided not to apply, it was due to a lot of unnecessary questions, which led me to doubt that the funds were legitimate in the first place.  
9. Would you want an agent/API interface, a website, or alerts? In what order?

	Alerts, Agents, Website (in that order)
