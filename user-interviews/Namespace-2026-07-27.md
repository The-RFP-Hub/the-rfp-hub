## Responses (verbatim)

**1. Walk me through how you currently find funding opportunities. What tools, what cadence?**

Mostly fragmented. I browse X, I have some Discord channels (Giveth for example), and word-of-mouth occasionally. I'm subscribed to a few grant programs directly via email. Occasionally I'll have my Hermes agent do a deep dive research of active opportunities in the market when I'm actively looking.

**2. Where does that process break down? What costs you the most time?**

Verification is bigger problem than discovery sometimes. Whether it's a fit or not. Scrolling through cluttered, noisy websites when I care about like 3 things only (budget, eligibility, deadlines, maybe something else but that's it). Also, filling out unnecessary long applications, writing updates after that, breaking down budget into milestones is the biggest turn off ever.

**3. What information is most often missing or wrong in opportunity listings?**

If opportunity is active or not. Eligibility - if we're a fit or not. A person to ping if we have extra questions. Or AI to ping if we have extra questions 😂

**4. If a neutral, open hub existed, what would it need for you to use it weekly?**

I'd subscribe and want a weekly digest. But I'm not sure if I'd visit the site.. maybe if it had a short-update feed like a timeline of short status updates per opportunity so I can catch up quickly when I check in. Dunno.

**5. Which fields actually matter to you in a listing?**

Eligibility, budget size, deadlines. If we're a fit. I've recently spent an hour digging into an opportunity that turned out to be for Solana-only projects. Also, contact person or AI agent. And when it comes to budget, I don't want to see - Budget 100k, and I go to apply, and upper limit is 2k.

**6. Would you contribute data back (submissions, corrections, flags)? What would make that worth doing?**

I'd be down to do this I don't think I need a reason. I'd be making the app better so why not. Maybe some free AI credits to use in the app (aayyyy).

**7. How do you find opportunities today? What tools do you use?**

Duplicate of Q1. Cap: "Already replied this."

**8. How do you decide whether an opportunity is worth applying to? What kills interest?**

Amount of money they give - that also kills interest if the amount is too small. Strategic alignment (can they help me beyond just money). What kills interest is generic nonsense spouting and not being straight to the point. Also, things that kill interest (and are annoying to see) are unnecessarily long applications and breaking down budget based on the milestone.

**9. Would you want an agent/API interface, a website, or alerts? In what order?**

API/agent yes. If the data is accessible programmatically, I can build my own alerts in whatever format, cadence, filter, etc. that I want. Simple generic alerts are ok too. Website is last because I personally won't check a dashboard regularly.
