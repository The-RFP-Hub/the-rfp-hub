Any signed-in account can file a claim for an existing organization, not only its members. A non-member's claim always queues for a reviewer; approving it also adds the claimant as a publisher of the organization. The claim form offers the listing's organizations or a typed slug, the review queue flags claimants who are not yet members, and one account can hold at most 10 pending claims.
