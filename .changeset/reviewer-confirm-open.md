Reviewers can keep a live listing open by hand. `POST /v1/review/opportunities/:id/confirm-open` (optional body `{"note": "…"}`, at most 500 characters) sets `last_seen_at` to now under a row lock and audits it as an `update` with `lastSeenAt` before/after and the note (or a default) as `reason`. Nothing else changes — not `updated_at`, not the content, not the reviewed link pins — and anything that is not approved, listed, open and unmerged is a `409` (`not_approved`, `not_listed`, `not_open`, `opportunity_merged`), as is a past-due entry (`past_due`) and one whose close is set by its final fixed deadline (`closes_on_deadline`): a confirmation would not keep either open any longer. `GET /v1/review/closing-soon?days=30&limit=100` lists the approved, listed, open entries the staleness job would close for inactivity within the window, soonest first, each with its `closesAt`, computed by the job's own rule (now shared in `modules/shared/staleness.ts`) projected across the window: a rolling programme whose last fixed deadline passes inside the window is listed at the later of that deadline and its inactivity date, while a past-due entry and one whose last fixed deadline is beyond the window are not. The items are the true soonest, read from one snapshot (`complete: false` if a safety cap on rows examined ever stops the search early), and each says by `closesBy` whether a confirmation can move its close. `GET /v1/opportunities/:id?count=false` serves the record without recording a detail view, for reads such as the frontend's leaving page that are not somebody reading the listing. No migration.
