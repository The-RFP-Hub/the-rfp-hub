How it works

Who's funding what on Ethereum, and where to apply.

RFP Hub lists grants, hackathons, bounties and RFPs from any organization building on Ethereum. We don't rank programs, review applications or decide who gets funded. We point you to the ones that are open.

What it is

A public index, not a portal

Every listing points back to the program's own site. The data is open: exported nightly as JSON and CSV under CC0, and readable through the API.

Any organization can be listed, and verified publishers manage their own listings directly. No one here decides who gets funded.
